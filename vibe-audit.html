What this app is

Goal. Ship an admin dashboard for PawsForward so operators can track donations, hire fundraisers with unique QR codes (25% of attributed donations owed), and see visit geography — plus a public donate page those QR codes open.

Who it is for. Organization admins who hire and pay QR fundraisers, and the public donors who give through those links.

Checks that fit. Present: public donate page, admin dashboard (private by intent), forms, Supabase database, first-party visit/geo analytics, operational statistics, daily admin UI, demo donation recording (not card payments). Not needed: blog, AI, native apps, uploads, marketing email, npm dependency tree, real payment processor flows. Password account UX does not exist yet, but the open admin still needs route protection and closed data access.

Based onadmin.html, donate.html, index.html, css/site.css, js/admin.js, js/donate.js, js/config.js, js/supabase-client.js, js/qr.js, js/ui-select.js, supabase/schema.sql, .gitignore, .env.example
