What this app is

Goal. Ship a PawsForward admin console and public donate page so operators can track donations, hire employees with unique QR codes (25% of attributed donations owed), and see visit geography after consent.

Who it is for. Organization admins who manage QR fundraisers, and donors who give through employee links.

Checks that fit. Present: public donate page, admin login/private dashboard, forms, Supabase database with RPCs, consent-gated visit analytics, operational stats, daily admin UI, demo donations (not card payments). Not needed: blog, AI, native apps, uploads, marketing email, npm dependency tree, real payment processor flows.
