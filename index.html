Who we are

A dog-rescue fundraising organization

Paws Forward raises money for dogs that need the basics — food, water, and shelter. We turn everyday sharing into real support: people create a personal donation QR, friends give through Stripe, and every attributed gift helps the rescue move forward.

Half of each gift that comes through a QR stays with the rescue. The other half goes to the person who shared it — so helping dogs and earning for spreading the word happen together.

How it works

  1. 1
    Create your QR

    Make a personal donation link in about a minute.

  2. 2
    Share it

    Friends scan, give any amount on Stripe, and the gift is tracked to you.

  3. 3
    Dogs get help — you earn 50%

    Half of every attributed gift supports the rescue; half is owed to you.

Questions

How do donations work?

You choose any amount on Stripe Checkout. The gift supports food, water, and shelter for dogs.

What is a promoter QR?

A personal link and QR that attributes gifts to you. Unless we publish a different rate, you earn 50% of attributed donations.

Do I need a QR to donate?

No. Direct donations on the Donate page support Paws Forward without promoter attribution.

Who processes payments and data?

Stripe processes payments. Supabase stores application data for profiles, gifts, and attribution. See our privacy policy for details.
