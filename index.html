How it works

  1. Create your QR. Make a personal donation link in about a minute. Name is optional, phone and payout details are enough.
  2. Share it. Friends scan, give any amount on Stripe, and the gift is tracked to your code.
  3. Dogs get help, you earn 50%. Half of every attributed gift supports the rescue; half is owed to you.

Why Paws Forward?

  1. Support for dogs. Reward for sharing.

    We raise money for dogs that need food, water, and shelter, and turn everyday sharing into real support.

  2. A clear 50 / 50 split.

    Half of every gift through a QR stays with the rescue. The other half goes to the person who shared it.

  3. Simple Stripe checkout.

    Donors give any amount. No account required to donate. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.
