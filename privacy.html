Privacy policy

PawsForward is a dog-rescue fundraising tool. This policy explains what we collect on the donate page and admin dashboard.

Who we are

PawsForward operates this site to raise funds for dogs that need food, water, and shelter, and to track employee QR fundraising. Contact: privacy@pawsforward.org.

What we collect

  • Donations (demo). Amount, optional donor name, employee referral code, and time.
  • Visits (with consent). Page path, approximate city/region/country from an IP lookup, referral code, and time.
  • Employees (admin). First name, last name, phone, preferred payment type, and referral code.
  • Deletion requests. Email and optional details you send through this page.
  • Admin accounts. Email used to sign in to the dashboard.

Why and who receives it

We use this data to record gifts, attribute fundraising to employees (25% of attributed donations for payouts), improve location support insights, and run the organization. Data is stored in Supabase (hosted Postgres). Approximate location uses ipapi.co when visit analytics are accepted.

Cookies and analytics

Essential theme preference may be stored locally. Visit and geo analytics run only after you accept on the donate page. You can refuse; donations still work.

Delete your personal data

Email privacy@pawsforward.org or submit a request below. We will delete or anonymize personal records we hold about you where we can identify them.

Breach notice

If personal data is breached, we will notify affected people using the contact details we have and document the incident per our breach plan.
