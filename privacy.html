Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Paws Forward (“we”) is a dog-rescue fundraising organization. This policy explains what information we collect on joinpawsforward.com and related pages, how we use it, and how you can reach us.

What we collect

  • Promoter profiles. When you create a QR, we store your name, phone number, payout type, payout handle, and a referral code.
  • Donations. Stripe processes payments. We store donation amount, time, optional donor label, attribution to a referral code when present, and Stripe session identifiers needed to confirm payment.
  • Visit / scan data. Opening a QR or donate link may record a visit with referral code, path, time, and coarse location fields when available.
  • Technical data. Standard server and browser logs (IP address, user agent) may be processed by our hosts (for example GitHub Pages and Supabase).
  • Operator access. Dashboard access uses a session PIN stored only in your browser session after unlock — we do not collect account emails on the public site for that gate.

How we use information

  • To attribute donations to the promoter whose QR was shared.
  • To pay out commissions and operate the rescue fundraising program.
  • To improve reliability, prevent abuse, and confirm Stripe payments.
  • To respond to privacy or support requests you send us.

Who processes data for us

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Supabase — database and edge functions.
  • GitHub Pages (or our domain host) — static site hosting.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data with processors needed to run payments and hosting, and when required by law.

Retention

Donation and promoter records are kept as long as needed for accounting, payouts, and legal obligations. Visit logs may be retained for analytics and fraud prevention, then deleted or aggregated.

Your choices

Email privacy@pawsforward.org to ask what we hold about you, correct it, or request deletion. We may need to verify your request and keep limited records where the law requires it.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit children’s personal information.

Contact
