PawsForward

Terms of service

These terms cover the public donate page, employee QR links, and the admin dashboard.

Mission

PawsForward raises support for dogs that need food, water, and shelter. Every recorded dollar is intended to help that mission.

Demo donations

The donate form currently records gifts for tracking and attribution. Card processing may be added later. A demo donation is not a completed bank or card charge until a processor is connected.

Employee QR fundraising

Employees receive unique QR codes. Donations through a code are attributed to that employee for a 25% payout of attributed donation totals, paid by PawsForward according to the employee’s preferred payment type. Misuse of codes (fraud, spam, or misleading solicitations) may result in removal.

Admin access

The admin dashboard is for authorized operators only. You must keep credentials private and sign out on shared devices.

Acceptable use

Do not abuse forms, scrape personal data, attempt to bypass access controls, or use the service for anything other than legitimate fundraising for dogs in need.

Contact

Questions: hello@pawsforward.org. Privacy requests: privacy@pawsforward.org or the form on the privacy policy.
