# Stripe business category checklist

Before taking live donations, confirm in the Stripe Dashboard that this fundraising model is allowed for the connected account.

## Confirm
1. Open Stripe Dashboard → Settings → Business settings / Public details.
2. Check the business category / statement descriptor matches a dog-rescue or charitable fundraising use case you disclosed.
3. Read Stripe’s restricted businesses list for your country and confirm fundraising / nonprofit donation collection is permitted for your account type.
4. If Stripe asks for nonprofit documentation, upload what they request before scaling volume.
5. Keep live charges off until Stripe approves the account for this activity.

## This product
- Paws Forward collects donations via Stripe Checkout (custom amount).
- Some gifts are attributed to promoter QR codes with a commission split.
- Do not describe the product as an investment, gambling, or unrelated restricted category.

Update this note when the Stripe account category is confirmed.
